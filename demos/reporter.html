Automated Retail Analytics Reporter

Raw retail orders go in; cleaned data, five headline KPIs and a branded PDF report come out. Pick a date range, watch the KPIs and charts recalculate, then download the report.

About this demo: a lightweight in-browser version of the Python pipeline (pandas, matplotlib, fpdf2, GitHub Actions). The KPI formulas and cleaning rules mirror kpi_engine.py and cleaner.py. The data is synthetic, generated in your browser by a seeded random generator, so numbers are deterministic but not real sales. The repo's sample CSV has no Region column; the region filter, monthly trend chart and a few deliberately dirty rows are demo additions. “Download PDF” uses your browser's print dialog (choose Save as PDF), not fpdf2. The scheduled run below is a simulation: nothing is scheduled or emailed.

What follows the repo, and what doesn't

  • Cleaning (cleaner.py): non-numeric Sales become the median Sales; missing Quantity becomes 1; Profit Margin = Profit ÷ Sales (Sales of 0 treated as 1); Month and Year derived from Order Date. Cleaning runs on the full dataset before filtering.
  • KPIs (kpi_engine.py): Total Revenue = Σ Sales, Total Profit = Σ Profit, Avg Order Value = mean Sales per row, Top Category = category with the highest Σ Sales, Total Orders = row count (each row is one order).
  • Not in this demo: reading the CSV from disk, matplotlib/fpdf2 rendering, Gmail delivery and a real scheduler. Real figures would come from your own data.
