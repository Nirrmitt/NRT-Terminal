About this demo

This is a lightweight in-browser version running on sample data (or whatever you upload or paste, which stays on your device). It mirrors the repo's pipeline shape: extract text per page, chunk by sentences, score chunks against the question, return page-cited passages.

  • Same idea: page-aware chunking on sentence boundaries, term-count cosine similarity over a simple word tokenizer (the "Raw term-count cosine" option follows the repo's keyword scorer), and results that carry document title and page number.
  • Added for the demo: BM25 and TF-IDF ranking so you can compare lexical methods, plus stop-word removal and light stemming in those two modes. Chunks here are about 400 characters so short sample pages split into a few passages; the repo's default is larger.
  • Not in this page: the full project also scores passages with sentence-transformer embeddings (BGE), blends them with keyword similarity, can re-rank with a cross-encoder, and generates grounded answers through an LLM behind a FastAPI service. None of that runs here: no embeddings, no LLM, no server, no API keys. Results are ranked passages only, not generated answers.
  • The scores are relative lexical scores on a tiny corpus. They are not relevance probabilities, and no accuracy figures are claimed.
